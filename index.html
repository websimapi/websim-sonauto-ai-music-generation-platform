About Sonauto AI

                    

                        

                            
What is Sonauto AI?

                            
Sonauto AI is a powerful music generation platform that leverages artificial intelligence to create high-quality, custom music based on your specifications. Whether you need music for videos, games, podcasts, or personal enjoyment, Sonauto AI can generate original tracks in seconds.

                            
                            
How It Works

                            
Our AI has been trained on thousands of songs across various genres. You can guide the generation process by providing tags, lyrics, or plain text prompts describing the music you want. The AI will then create a unique track based on your input.

                            
                            
API Credits

                            
One 1 minute 35 second song costs 100 credits. Generating 2 songs in one request costs 150 credits. Visit the Sonauto website for more information about pricing plans.
